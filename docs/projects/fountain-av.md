# Fountain Avenue (Hollywood / West Hollywood)

Compiled 2026-11-17 from web search result summaries only (LADOT and WeHo sites
could not be fetched directly). Every fact below should be re-checked against the
linked source before publication.

## Summary

The Fountain Avenue "road diet" that appears in the press is **not an LADOT project**.
It is the **City of West Hollywood's Fountain Avenue Streetscape Project**, on the part
of Fountain west of La Brea Av. Phase 1 (four lanes to two, protected bike lanes) was
approved by the West Hollywood City Council on 2025-09-15 [BP-2025-09] and, as of the
latest reporting found, construction was anticipated to start in fall 2026 but was
**not confirmed as started** [BP-2026-09][LAist-vote]. In the City of Los Angeles
(La Brea Av east to Sunset Bl), no LADOT lane-reduction project on Fountain was found.
The only LADOT work found on Fountain itself is a 2023 westbound bike lane in East
Hollywood (Vermont Av to Kingsley Dr) [SBLA-2023]. Other LADOT projects only touch
Fountain as an end point (Hobart Bl lanes, Hollywood Bl phase 2, Sunset/Cesar Chavez).

## What is installed or planned

West Hollywood Phase 1 [BP-2025-09][LAist]:
- Travel lanes reduced from four to one in each direction (two of four lanes removed).
- Protected bike lanes in both directions.
- 210 on-street parking spaces removed [BP-2025-09].
- High-visibility crosswalks and curb extensions.
- Resurfacing and restriping.
- Signal changes: not found (a separate signal improvement job at Fountain/Fairfax
  appears in a bid listing; relation to this project not confirmed).

West Hollywood Phase 2 [BP-2026-09][BP-2025-09]: full reconstruction, wider sidewalks,
landscaping, curb ramps and sidewalks brought to ADA compliance. Phase 1 is meant to
inform its final design.

LADOT 2023 [SBLA-2023]: one westbound bike lane, Vermont Av to Kingsley Dr; eastbound
remains sharrows. No lane removal was reported in the summary found.

## Published data

- Collisions (WeHo segment): 35 severe-injury collisions and 5 fatal collisions,
  2013 to 2024, per the city staff report [LAist][BP-2025-09].
- State grant at risk if the scope changes: nearly $5 million [LAist-vote].
- Speed surveys: not found.
- Traffic counts: not found.
- Before/after data: not found (project not built).
- LADOT data for the Los Angeles segment: not found.

## Bus service

- West Hollywood segment (La Cienega Bl to La Brea Av): no Metro line or DASH route
  running along Fountain was found. Not positively confirmed from a route map; WeHo's
  own Cityline shuttle was not checked.
- City of Los Angeles segment: **LADOT DASH Hollywood does use Fountain Av.** Its stop
  list includes Fountain/Vine (terminal), Fountain & Seward, Fountain & Las Palmas and
  Highland & Fountain [DASH][Transit]. DASH Hollywood/Wilshire lists a Gower St &
  Fountain Av stop [TW-HW]. So "no bus service" holds only for the West Hollywood part.
- Metro bus running along Fountain: not found (Metro lines cross it on north-south
  streets).

## Unverified or conflicting

- **Which project the owner means.** No LADOT "Fountain Av road diet" was found. Either
  the owner means the West Hollywood project, or an LADOT project exists that search
  did not surface (ladotlivablestreets.org listings could not be read).
- **Has construction started?** Sources through September 2026 say "anticipated this
  fall". No report of ground-breaking or a contract award was found.
- **Completion date.** Reported as winter 2027 in one summary and spring 2027
  (16 to 18 month timeline) in another [BP-2025-09][BP-2025-08].
- **3-2 vote date.** LAist says the delay motion failed "Monday night"; the date was
  not in the summary [LAist-vote].
- **Court ruling.** A BikinginLA page says a Superior Court judge ruled the bike lanes
  can go forward; date and case not found [BLA].
- **Bus claim.** DASH Hollywood on Fountain between about Highland Av and Vine St
  conflicts with "no bus service" if the study area includes Hollywood.
- **2023 LADOT lane.** Whether it removed a travel lane or parking was not stated.
- Coordinates in `corridors/fountain.json` are approximate and not surveyed.

# Hollywood Boulevard Safety and Mobility Project (LADOT)

Researched 2026-11-17. Method note: the official project page
(ladotlivablestreets.org/projects/hollywood-blvd) and the LADOT fact sheet PDF
could not be opened from this machine. Everything below comes from web-search
result summaries of the pages listed under Sources; no source page was read in
full. Treat quoted figures as needing a check against the original page.

## Summary

The Hollywood Boulevard Safety and Mobility Project is an LADOT quick-build
street redesign in Hollywood / East Hollywood / Los Feliz (Council District 13)
intended to reduce severe and fatal crashes [LADOT news]. Phase 1, on Hollywood
Bl between Gower St and Lyman Pl (reported as Hillhurst Av by news outlets),
was built starting August 2024 and its protected bike lanes opened that month
[HUNC 31139; LAist "are here"; ABC7]. Phase 2, about a quarter mile along
Sunset Bl from Lyman Pl to Fountain Av, is still in design, with a stated goal
of implementation in early 2027 [HUNC 34725]. No evidence was found that
Phase 2 has been built.

## Phase 1: Gower St to Lyman Pl / Hillhurst Av

- Extents: Hollywood Bl from Gower St to Lyman Pl [LADOT news]. News coverage
  gives the east end as Hillhurst Av and the length as "just over two miles" /
  2.1 miles [LAist "are here"; ABC7; Urbanize].
- Lane changes: one travel lane in each direction converted to a
  parking-protected bike lane between Lyman Pl and Van Ness Av; between Van
  Ness Av and Gower St, parking replaced with protected bike lanes
  (plastic delineators) [LADOT news; Urbanize].
- Centre lane: along part of the corridor a travel lane in each direction was
  converted into a continuous centre left-turn lane [HUNC 31139].
- Other elements: painted curb extensions and "additional quick-build
  features" [Streetsblog 2024-03-21].
- Bus boarding islands: a vendor page (Zicla "Zipper system Hollywood
  Boulevard") indicates modular separator/platform products on the corridor;
  count and locations not found.
- Signal changes in Phase 1: not found.
- Described as the first protected bike lanes in Council District 13
  [LAist "are here"].

Key dates

- August 2023: LADOT launched the project / began outreach [LADOT news;
  Spectrum 2023-08-25].
- 2024-02-05: Streetsblog LA reports the city is planning protected bike lanes

- August 2024: lanes opened, ribbon-cutting held [LAist "are here"; ABC7;

- Formal completion date of all Phase 1 elements: not found.

Cost and funding

## Phase 2: Lyman Pl to Fountain Av along Sunset Bl

- Extents: from Lyman Pl to Fountain Av along Sunset Bl, roughly a quarter
  mile [LADOT news; Streetsblog 2024-05-30].
- Focus: the six-way intersection of Hollywood Bl, Sunset Bl, Hillhurst Av,
  Virgil Av and Sunset Dr (by the Vista Theatre) [LADOT news; LAist
  "dangerous intersection"].
- Proposed: changes to left-turn signal phasing in certain directions;
  removal of parking on Sunset Bl between Hillhurst Av and Fountain Av to
  connect to the existing Sunset bike lanes [LADOT news].
- Status: LADOT calls the design a draft, subject to change for technical
  feasibility, and says Phase 2 "will be installed at a later date"
  [LADOT news]. A Hollywood United NC post says LADOT is "currently finalizing
  the design and schedule for Phase 2 with the goal of implementing this
  project in early 2027" and asks for feedback on the proposed design
  [HUNC 34725]. Date of that post: not found.
- Survey: the HUNC post invites feedback; the survey link, opening date and
  deadline were not found.
- Early outreach survey (project-wide): about 87% of respondents said
  improving safety on Hollywood Bl is very important [reported in search
  summary of Streetsblog/LADOT coverage; exact source page not confirmed].

## Evaluation and before/after figures

- No published LADOT before/after evaluation was found: no speed data, no
  collision comparison, no bike or pedestrian counts.
- Only outcome claim found: a Beverly Press article (September 2026) quotes
  Councilmember Hugo Soto-Martinez saying there have been zero fatal
  collisions on the stretch since the redesign. Not verified against LADOT or
  SWITRS data.
- Anecdotal: Spectrum News (2024-08-29) quoted experts saying the lanes are
  slowing the street; KTLA reported complaints of backups. No figures.
- Related, not an evaluation: Council File 25-0558, LADOT report dated
  2026-01-23, on camera enforcement of bike lanes on Hollywood Bl. Not read.

## Collision statistics cited for the corridor

- 53 severe-injury and fatal collisions in the project area, 2010-2019,
  cited by an LADOT planner [LAist "dangerous intersection"].
- About 56 people killed or severely injured on the same stretch over the
  same period, per Councilmember Soto-Martinez's office [same search
  summary; exact page not confirmed].
- Hollywood Bl is on the High Injury Network [LADOT news].

## Unverified or conflicting

- East terminus: LADOT says Lyman Pl; news outlets say Hillhurst Av. These
  are adjacent at the six-way junction and likely describe the same point.
- Casualty figure: 53 collisions vs about 56 people. Different units
  (collisions vs victims), so both may be correct.
- Phase 2 definition: LADOT and Streetsblog define it as Sunset Bl to
  Fountain Av; one TV report describes a second phase running west to the
  Walk of Fame and Orange Dr, which appears to be the separate Access to
  Hollywood plan.
- "Launched August 2023" (planning/outreach) vs "construction August 2024":
  not a conflict, but easy to confuse.
- Bus boarding islands, turn pockets and signal changes for Phase 1: asked
  for in the brief, not confirmed by any source summary beyond the items
  listed above.
- Zero fatal collisions since redesign: single political source, unverified.
